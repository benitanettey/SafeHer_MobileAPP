# 🚨 SafeHer - Offline Safety App for Women

Java Android | APK Download | Kenya-Focused

SafeHer is a **preventive safety mobile application** designed for women in Kenya, enabling **instant emergency response, offline protection, and secure communication** in high-risk situations.

---

## 📋 Problem Statement

Gender-based violence in Kenya continues to rise, especially affecting women in vulnerable environments:

* 🚨 Delayed emergency response in critical situations
* 📵 No internet access during emergencies
* 📍 Difficulty sharing real-time location quickly
* 🔐 Lack of privacy in existing safety tools
* ⚠️ Over-reliance on reactive (not preventive) systems

---

## 🎯 Our Solution

SafeHer provides a **reliable, offline-first safety system** that works even in low-connectivity environments:

* 📡 **Offline SMS SOS alerts** to trusted contacts
* 📍 **GPS location sharing** without internet
* 🔐 **Encrypted contact storage** for privacy
* ⏱️ **Safety check-ins** to prevent escalation
* 📴 Fully functional even without mobile data

## 🎥 How It Works - Step by Step

### Step 1: Install & Setup

Download the APK and install the app. Set up your secure PIN and add trusted emergency contacts.

### Step 2: Add Trusted Contacts

Input contacts who will receive SOS alerts. These are stored securely on your device.

### Step 3: Enable Safety Check-ins

Set a timer (e.g., when traveling). If you don’t check in, SafeHer automatically triggers alerts.

### Step 4: Trigger SOS

In danger? Tap the SOS button. SafeHer instantly sends an SMS with your location to all trusted contacts.

### Step 5: Location Sharing

Your GPS coordinates are attached to messages, allowing contacts to track your last known location.

### Step 6: Stay Protected Offline

Even without internet or data, SMS alerts and safety features continue to function.
